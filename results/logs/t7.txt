Hierarchy kept: CSLA is 27% faster (1.242 vs 1.710 ns) but 1.75x the area, so ADP is worse (293.7 vs 231.1 um2.ns).
Flattened: ABC cuts area (CSLA -52%, RCA -19%) and slows both (CSLA +28%, RCA +17% delay). Flat CSLA still beats flat RCA (1.595 vs 2.005 ns) and has the best ADP (179.6 vs 218.3).
Flat critical paths end in small o21ai_0 gates after two maj3 stages; the hierarchical fa is pinned to maj3_1 + xor3_1.
Hypotheses (not checked): ABC maps for area first and trades speed; 4 bits is short, so duplicated hi blocks cost more area than the mux saves in delay.
Final designs use the hierarchical form (Task 6, D-6), so the CSLA speed-up is real but costs area.
